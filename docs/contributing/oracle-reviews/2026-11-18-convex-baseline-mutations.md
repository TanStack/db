# Convex accepted baseline: bounded mutation evidence

The final source preserves accepted saved rows until provider evidence ends
them. A finite lazy prefix has authority over its established range; an
exhausted answer has authority over the whole query. Accepted but unapplied
rows, queued provider proposals, and later hydration must participate in that
judgment. The SQLite persistence guide's "On-demand Collections offline"
contract and the primary Convex lazy models supply the laws. Production output
does not supply the expected answer.

[The evidence manifest](2026-11-18-convex-baseline-mutation-evidence.json)
records all source variants, commands, raw logs, structured results and
SHA-256s. Its final production baseline is `options.ts` **872d94be…**,
`pagination.ts` **1328c4bf…**, and `subscription.ts` **375baafa…**. All three
were restored byte for byte after every mutation. The 28 frozen instrument,
runtime-helper and dependency fingerprints stayed unchanged throughout the
campaign. Installed dependencies were not patched.

## GREEN and measured receiving reach

On that exact baseline, the common persisted-demand receiver passes **30/30
Node SQLite and 30/30 Chromium/OPFS** checks. The larger saved-peer receiver
passes **12/12 per host**. The controlled lazy-baseline owner passes **80/80**.
The three completed initial baseline profiles and the remaining paired-run
profiles form this baseline; an interrupted initial peer-browser attempt is
not counted as evidence.

Each host's peer grammar enumerates eight histories: source size eight/nine,
changed/unchanged prefix values, and distinct/tied boundary ranks. All eight
reach each of five common states; four reach the tied-boundary state. The
larger exact demand saves five rows online. After close/reopen, a smaller
demand receives a finite four-row prefix while a real optimistic transaction
holds application. The larger saved suffix is accepted but unapplied, its raw
provider has delivered no answer, and the public base still holds only the
smaller window. After release, source rows, all authored durable rows and the
larger demand's offline readiness compare with the unchanged primary
persistence model. The original peer report explains this receiving boundary.

## Survivors and excluded evidence

The public-base mutant **survives all 12 saved-peer checks on both hosts**,
including the changed and tied cells. That receiver does not distinguish this
projection error; the kill above belongs only to the controlled owner. A
suffix outside the current prefix need not affect the provider's current
scope. This remains a precise limit of the receiving evidence.

Ignoring proposal tombstones **survives all ten deferred-owner checks**. In
this successful FIFO, full-row, no-metadata domain, replacing a missing row
with a full-row update is allowed by the persisted-source contract, as
recorded in `pr-1902-change-event-history.md:241`. The survivor therefore does
not justify inventing a write-kind law. It also does not prove equivalence
under canceled acceptance, sync metadata, or arbitrary histories.

The first repair's additional controlled run has 51 pass and 29 failures
while establishing late-hydration/reference-identity premises. Those are not
credited as kills; its actual saved-peer comparisons supply the semantic RED.
The original-startup Chromium attempt and first warm retry fail at Vite/browser
startup before histories. A second retry completes with the same 12 semantic
failures as Node. Setup failures remain in the manifest as setup evidence.
Derivative reach/calibration failures are likewise not additional kills.

Default logs preserve nested `OracleViolation`, checkpoint and `Difference`
causes that the structured Vitest JSON omits. The manifest's manual semantic
categories use those default logs rather than the runner's provisional labels.

## Limits

The tied-boundary witness is ascending; this campaign does not establish a
separate descending-tie kill. The evidence does not cover deployed Convex
services, arbitrary predicates/cursors, retention expiry, concurrent tabs, or
whole-adapter completeness. Historical source variants are exact recorded
file replacements against the final baseline, not clean historical checkouts.
The earlier peer candidate report remains attributed to **690a8ed3…**; the
GREEN and mutations in this report apply to **872d94be…**.

The source mutation lock was released only after exact restoration and the
unchanged-instrument check. Further oracle review and whole-branch integration
remain with the coordinating task.
