## Raw state14 and proposed legal-history clarification

Preserve the raw label exactly:
`unexpected-retained-target-receipt-rejection`.

Proposed recipe lane: `cleanup-abandonment`.
Proposed comparison cut: `retained-cleanup-abandoned`.
The baseline constituent uses `cleanup-abandoned`. This is a proposed
legal-history clarification pending independent source review and maintainer
adoption of the mapping. It does not rename the original inventory, adopt a
new product rejection policy, retire a law, or claim raw14 reach.

Architecture law7 permits an applied receipt to reject with AbortError when
cleanup abandons its accepted transaction. Its ordinary accepted transaction
otherwise applies in commit order. The required measured order is:

1. An actual exact retained-target commit returns a non-literal pending receipt
   and records its acceptance.
2. Cleanup start invalidates that sync run.
3. That original receipt rejects with AbortError after cleanup start.
4. Complete cleanup permits restart. A new operation and current task belong to
   the new sync run; old receipt/task delivery cannot change them.
5. The current operation makes positive progress and completes with its chosen
   coherent public window after its own obligations and whole tail succeed.

The checker requires `acceptedAt < cleanupStartedAt < rejectedAt`, a retained
abandoned output tied to the retired run, and the actual AbortError identity
classification. An arbitrary live accepted-receipt rejection, an invalidated
pre-acceptance open transaction, or a fixture Promise rejected by hand cannot
supply this state. Merely proving the old callback did nothing does not prove
positive current-run progress. The stale-task cut and final current-operation
check supply that separate proposed obligation.
