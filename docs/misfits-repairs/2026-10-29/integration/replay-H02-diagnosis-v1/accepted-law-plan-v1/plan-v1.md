## Bounded progress comparison and evidence classification

A same-thread timer cannot interrupt the synchronous graph drain. Use an
external supervisor and primitive durable phase receipts. The parent-side
progress check must consume a reached-premise receipt and distinguish queued
from executed; it may then report a bounded progress failure if the operational
watchdog expires. Do not pretend the aborted worker ran its final semantic
comparison. Neither a timeout nor an OOM proves infinite execution.

Classify every run as setup/import failure, unmet premise, resource termination,
progress watchdog after the measured queued-answer premise, callback execution
followed by semantic assertion failure, or complete GREEN. A general runtime
failure is not progress RED. Event128 termination in the old diagnostics is
not RED, receiving reach, retention comparison, or closure evidence.

Review the new oracle/instrument before using it to change production. Show
the generated premise and bounded progress RED on selected unchanged current
production. After an authorized repair, use the identical instrument and
histories GREEN. Restore the current original behavior as a hostile mutant and
require the intended progress failure after the same premise. Keep timeout
classification separate from semantic-comparison rejection, as ORC-006 requires.

Challenge plausible wrong repairs with existing/new receiving controls:
discarding cached rows must fail ownership; a partial replacement must fail
retained publication; suppressing pivot demand must fail independent demand
coverage/final values; blanket yielding must fail the literal-true initial and
ordinary synchronous mutation cuts. The oracle campaign must retain fixed and
unseeded runs plus direct seed/path replay. Pick and register its budget with
the owner; do not lower existing budgets to fit this work.
