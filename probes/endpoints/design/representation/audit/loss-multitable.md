## Recovered readout

The hidden losses are distinct from the acknowledged implementation scope:

1. **Roles collapsed:** mutation reads versus writes (MT03), static possible writes versus executed writes (MT04), and changing permissions versus static scope identity (MT13).
2. **Histories collapsed:** a single endpoint may span several commits or services (MT11). The draft's explicit read-error outcome begins only after a successfully returned mutation callback; the reduction does not state what a failed callback says about prior committed steps.
3. **Support boundaries thinned:** maintainable loaded joins (MT06), absence-sensitive dependencies (MT08), aggregate-specific support (MT16), and simultaneous join-input cross-terms (MT17). The latter query forms are explicitly deferred in the executable slice, but their distinct source laws are missing from the grammar's generic support condition.
4. **Cost and coverage detail compressed:** unavoidable join fanout (MT15) and the source's concrete missing oracle dimensions (MT19).

Other central conditions remain visible: conservative unknown-effect fallback, insufficient changed-row support, actual-versus-guessed authority, transaction-owned retirement, safe scoped identity, pending activation limits, and separate database/client observation boundaries.

S does not select a query-generality limit, optimism display policy, ownership form, or repair plan (S:61,84,88). The draft selects separate results, one Todo schema, nonempty coherent guesses and stable loaded demand (D:15,51–55). Those are explicit implementation restrictions; they must not be attributed to source-established impossibility. This audit ends at supported source-to-reduction traces.
