# Donor Perturbation: Trace Coordination, Scoped Marks, and Gated Selection

## Instrument boundary

This reading transfers mechanisms into the frozen TanStack Trust API grammar without selecting an API configuration or revising the grammar. Three donors were recruited from a home-free abstract brief and researched in separate fresh contexts. The researchers did not see TanStack Trust, Endpoints, one another’s donors, or the later mapping.

The blind brief described autonomous actors with limited memory coordinating through persistent but fallible environmental changes; productive and pathological feedback; local adaptations that cannot authorize themselves; discoverable work; provenance; objection time; expiry; exceptions; and repair. The recruiter returned exactly three donors:

1. ant pheromone-trail recruitment, with tandem running as the nearby negative case;
2. urban search-and-rescue building markings, with live radio dispatch as the nearby negative case; and
3. germinal-center B-cell affinity maturation with T-follicular-helper gating, with T-independent activation as the nearby negative case.

Fit labels mean:

- `[fit: solid]`: the donor and Trust share the stated relation, and the mapping discriminates against the nearby negative case;
- `[fit: plausible]`: the relation is supported but changes medium, authority, or semantics during transfer; and
- `[fit: reach]`: the resemblance is useful to retain as a question or warning, but the donor does not establish the Trust mechanism.

## Home structure revealed after donor research

The frozen Trust grammar contains ten semantic primitives and seven active projections:

- stable entity references;
- domain packages;
- claims;
- support routes and claim uses;
- observations;
- evidence context and applicability;
- operation records;
- challenges and repair links;
- admission records;
- consumer policy profiles and overrides;
- evidence uses;
- assessment and explanation;
- policy-free conditions;
- work cases;
- diagnostics;
- policy evaluations; and
- coherent snapshots and cursors.

Its central authority boundary is fixed: domain packages own meaning, checks, adequacy, and applicability; Trust owns evidence mechanics, history, and shared operations; consumer policy owns severity, permission, fallback, and deployment action. Agents may propose every kind of object but may not admit their own semantic trust roots. Work state, notes, diagnostics, policy, and interface rendering do not count as evidence.

## Donor 1: ant pheromone-trail recruitment

### Donor mechanism

Ant trail recruitment is indirect, substrate-mediated coordination. A worker deposits a chemical trace on a route; later workers sense local concentration differences and alter their movement; successful traffic can reinforce the same path. Sender and receiver do not need to be present at the same time. The relevant state is distributed across trail geometry, chemical identity and concentration, age, traffic, resource state, worker memory, and environmental topology. [A1](#a1) [A3](#a3)

The dossier found several interacting controls:

- trail use and trail strength form a positive-feedback loop;
- chemical and behavioral effectiveness decay at species- and function-specific rates;
- some species combine long-lived exploratory signals with shorter-lived exploitation signals;
- population thresholds and hysteresis determine whether a trail can begin or persist;
- geometry and private memory can override or weaken pheromone bias;
- crowding and unsuccessful foragers can provide negative feedback and renewed exploration;
- repellent or no-entry pheromones can retire a branch faster than attraction decays; and
- public traces can be read by predators or experimentally replayed without authenticating their producer. [A1](#a1) [A4](#a4) [A5](#a5) [A6](#a6) [A7](#a7) [A8](#a8) [A10](#a10)

The failures are endogenous to the mechanism. Positive feedback can trap a colony on an obsolete or poor route, amplify an erroneous trace that cannot be erased quickly, or break symmetry arbitrarily. Army-ant mills show a stronger pathology: local agreement and continued motion can remain high while endpoint progress is zero. [A7](#a7) [A12](#a12) [A13](#a13)

### Candidate transfers

#### A-T1 — Conditions and work cases as environmental cues `[fit: solid]`

`TrustCondition` plus `WorkCase` has the same coordination job as a trace in a shared medium: one agent’s completed check, discovered counterexample, expiring observation, note, or claim on work changes state that a later agent can discover without sharing the first agent’s memory. `TrustSnapshot` and its cursor make that substrate inspectable across asynchronous sessions.

The transfer is deliberately narrower than “the evidence graph is pheromone.” An immutable observation is a record about a law and case. A condition is a derived cue that something needs attention. A work case adds coordination state. Those roles must remain separate.

#### A-T2 — Trace strength must not become evidence strength `[fit: solid]`

Ant recruitment becomes stronger when more ants traverse and reinforce a route. Trust must preserve the opposite epistemic rule: repeated work, many notes, many agents choosing an item, or a heavily linked task cannot make a claim supported. This donor strengthens the existing exclusion of work relations, closure, and popularity from evidence assessment.

The mapping is a negative constraint rather than an imported algorithm. Pheromone concentration is useful because it directs traffic; it is not a truth measure even in the donor domain.

#### A-T3 — Separate durable history from volatile attention state `[fit: plausible]`

Multi-timescale pheromones suggest keeping several lifetimes distinct:

- claim, observation, challenge, admission, and operation history remains append-only;
- an evidence use can become stale when its domain-owned context no longer matches;
- a condition can appear or disappear as assessment changes;
- a work claim or lease can expire so another agent may act; and
- consumer overrides have explicit expiry without changing the underlying evidence.

The analogy does not justify deleting observations on a clock or applying one global half-life. Trust freshness is driven by dependency revisions, applicability rules, challenge state, and domain semantics—not a generic evaporation constant.

#### A-T4 — Negative feedback and maintained exploration `[fit: plausible]`

Ant systems remain flexible when crowding, dissatisfied foragers, decay, memory, geometry, or repellent signals counter pure reinforcement. Nearby Trust mechanisms include versioned work claims, leases, blocker relations, supersession, ready-versus-blocked queries, expiry, and repeated gap discovery.

The donor raises a specific later-grammar question: whether work coordination needs explicit hold/objection state and a minimum objection interval before a coordinating proposal is treated as settled. It does not answer what that interval should be or whether every work case needs one.

#### A-T5 — Progress must be anchored outside the coordination loop `[fit: solid]`

An ant mill is active, locally coherent, and unproductive. The Trust analogue is a work graph with frequent claims, notes, reruns, or supersessions but no new admitted observation, applicability change, authorized repair, resolved challenge, or policy decision. Agent activity and queue throughput therefore cannot stand in for semantic progress.

A later API could expose a derived “condition unchanged” or “no evidence-state delta” fact, but the donor does not select its representation.

#### A-T6 — Public readability, write identity, and admission authority are separate `[fit: plausible]`

Pheromone trails are publicly readable within their environment and can be exploited or replayed. Trust similarly benefits from broad agent readability while requiring provenance for writes and external authority for admission. Authentication of an actor says who wrote a proposal or observation; it does not establish that the writer may admit a checker, rule, rubric, model, encoding, or domain package.

### Boundary and negative control

Tandem running is direct dyadic instruction: a leader and follower are co-present, continuously exchange contact signals, correct loss of contact immediately, and transfer route memory into the follower. It has no durable environmental route, anonymous aggregation, concentration field, passage reinforcement, or evaporation-based forgetting. [A9](#a9) [A16](#a16)

The pheromone mechanism therefore should **not** be used to specify direct request/response semantics, check invocation, cancellation, transport acknowledgment, or an editor’s immediate presentation of a diagnostic. Those are closer to tandem communication. The durable Trust condition behind the diagnostic may be stigmergic; the synchronous delivery exchange is not.

## Donor 2: urban search-and-rescue building markings

### Donor mechanism

Urban search-and-rescue teams attach large, standardized marks to damaged structures and worksites so later crews can discover what was assessed or searched, by whom, when, to what depth, with what hazards and findings, and whether more work remains. The mark is a low-bandwidth spatial record attached to its subject. [U1](#u1) [U2](#u2)

The FEMA and INSARAG systems are related but not identical. FEMA separates structural/hazard assessment from a two-stroke search mark: the first slash records entry and “search in progress”; the crossing slash records personnel exit, with team identity, times, hazards, and known victims around the X. INSARAG records a worksite ID, team ID, completed Assessment, Search and Rescue level, date, hazards, triage category, later teams’ deeper passes, and an explicit line through the full mark only when no further work is required. [U1](#u1) [U2](#u2)

The systems update by append, cross-out-and-replace, or a new marking when the old assessment becomes wrong. Reinspection is explicit when conditions, capability, access, time of day, or search depth changes. Maps, reports, coordination systems, briefings, and incident command retain information too rich for paint. Authority over the marking system, work allocation, escalation, rapid clearance, and end of the search phase lies with incident and jurisdictional roles rather than any crew that happens to make a mark. [U1](#u1) [U2](#u2)

### Candidate transfers

#### U-T1 — Stable subject attachment and provenance `[fit: solid]`

A condition or observation needs the equivalent of a conspicuous worksite address: a stable package-scoped subject reference, source location, law/check version, actor identity, and captured time/context. This supports the existing `EntityRef`, source locator, operation record, and snapshot design.

The physical donor adds a useful test: a record separated from its exact referent is unsafe even if its contents remain legible.

#### U-T2 — Completion must always name coverage depth `[fit: solid]`

“Searched” is too weak. INSARAG records the specific ASR level, and crossed-out victim counts refer to known victims at one marked location. A FEMA full X records team exit, not exhaustive clearance.

The Trust transfer is direct: a result must retain law, case, method, production path, checkpoint, observed fields, omissions, bounds, and context. A passed check is not proof of a larger claim, analyzer completeness, liveness, or absence of undiscovered counterexamples. The domain package defines what coverage means.

#### U-T3 — Reach, execution, observation, and clearance are distinct `[fit: solid]`

The two-stroke FEMA mark makes an unfinished operation visible; INSARAG separates completed search level from the later “no further work required” mark. This matches Trust’s separation among operation reach, process outcome, correctness observation, assessment, work closure, and consumer policy action.

The transfer sharpens one message rule: a diagnostic should be able to say “the check did not reach the observation point,” “the check reached it and observed an inconclusive result,” or “the claim is supported under this profile” without compressing them into “failed” or “green.”

#### U-T4 — Append and supersede; do not rewrite history `[fit: solid]`

Later teams add dated, identified assessments or replace an invalid current mark while leaving the history visible in reports. Conflicting field marks become an authority and reconciliation problem; they are not resolved safely by a later crew silently overwriting whichever mark it dislikes.

This maps to immutable observations, append-only challenges and repair attempts, versioned admission and policy records, optimistic concurrency for work, and explicit supersession. An incoming caller-owned object must not mutate stored history.

#### U-T5 — Reinspection follows changed conditions or capability `[fit: solid]`

INSARAG permits repeated triage when conditions or search capacity change and deeper passes as access improves. Trust’s equivalent is contextual applicability plus dependency revisions: previous evidence remains history, while a new use becomes stale, inapplicable, or unknown until the relevant check or judgment is repeated.

The donor does not establish which code, data, environment, method, or external changes are material. That remains domain-owned.

#### U-T6 — One visible mark is backed by richer parallel records `[fit: plausible]`

Paint supports fast local orientation; maps, forms, reports, coordination dashboards, and briefings carry detail and resolve ambiguity. LSP diagnostics can play the mark’s role: compact, source-attached, and actionable. MCP explanations, CLI JSON, operation history, and Devtools supply the richer records.

This is a projection relationship, not independent corroboration. All adapters may still share a defective semantic service.

#### U-T7 — Scoped operational authority `[fit: plausible]`

Field doctrine separates crew judgments, specialist hazard assessments, coordination, jurisdictional command, and the authority to introduce rapid clearance or end the US&R phase. The Trust relation is the separation among evidence producer, domain admission authority, work coordinator, and consumer-policy authorizer.

The analogy does not require Trust to prescribe an incident-command hierarchy or mandatory independent reviewer. It supports typed, inspectable authority scopes and explicit escalation when an actor lacks one.

### Boundary and negative control

Live radio dispatch uses standardized identity, location, condition, action, and need reports, but it is synchronous, capacity-constrained command traffic. Delivery, comprehension, acknowledgment, rebroadcast, channel allocation, preemption, and Mayday priority are central. Its later CAD or radio log is evidence about the exchange, not a substitute for the exchange. [U3](#u3)

The marking mechanism therefore should **not** govern Trust commands such as `checks.run`, observation submission, admission, cancellation, or policy activation. Those operations need request validation, delivery/receipt, authorization, cancellation, and transaction outcomes. The durable records they produce can use append-and-supersede semantics; the live command path cannot be modeled as “leave a mark and hope the intended actor eventually sees it.”

## Donor 3: germinal-center B-cell affinity maturation

### Donor mechanism

In a germinal center, activated B-cell lineages proliferate and use activation-induced cytidine deaminase to generate stochastic mutations in immunoglobulin variable regions. Mutated cells compete to capture native antigen displayed by follicular dendritic cells, process it, and present linked peptide on MHC-II. T-follicular-helper cells use a separately rearranged receptor to recognize that presentation and supply limiting CD40L/cytokine help. Positively selected cells may re-enter the mutation cycle or differentiate toward memory or antibody-secreting output; cells that receive no sufficient help die by default. [I1](#i1) [I2](#i2) [I3](#i3) [I4](#i4) [I5](#i5) [I6](#i6)

A receptor mutation changes a candidate’s binding behavior but cannot declare itself useful or durable. Selection depends on antigen availability and extraction, linked-peptide presentation, T-cell repertoire, competitor state, helper signals, regulatory cells, apoptosis, and later survival niches. The system is imperfect: somatic mutation can create autoreactivity or genomic damage; rare or inaccessible self-antigens may escape negative selection; excessive help or failed suppression can amplify nonspecific lineages; defective deletion can cause lymphoproliferation; and long-lived outputs may outlast the conditions that produced them. [I8](#i8) [I9](#i9) [I10](#i10) [I11](#i11) [I12](#i12) [I13](#i13) [I14](#i14) [I15](#i15) [I16](#i16)

### Candidate transfers

#### I-T1 — Generation, evaluation, admission, and retention are different operations `[fit: solid]`

Somatic mutation generates a candidate; antigen capture exposes behavior; helper recognition supplies a selection signal; differentiation and a survival niche create durable output. Trust needs the same categorical separation for agent participation:

- propose a claim, route, check, rule, interpretation, or repair;
- execute or inspect it and return observations;
- assess applicability and support under admitted domain semantics;
- admit or revoke semantic trust roots under external authority; and
- retain immutable history independently of current authority.

An agent-authored definition or proof can be valuable without gaining self-authorizing status.

#### I-T2 — Candidate content cannot contain its own verdict `[fit: solid]`

A mutated receptor changes what a cell can bind. It cannot state that the target is relevant, non-self, or worthy of memory. Likewise, a syntactically valid check, formal proof, rubric, model judgment, or claimed evidence route cannot determine its own adequacy, applicability, or admission. Provenance identifies the proposal; it does not supply its verdict.

This is the clearest donor support for the existing rule that agents may propose semantic material but cannot admit their own trust roots.

#### I-T3 — Evidence acquisition and applicability are linked but nonidentical tests `[fit: plausible]`

B-cell receptor binding to native antigen and T-cell recognition of processed linked peptide are separate gates. For Trust, an observation can be authentic and correctly produced yet still be inapplicable to a requested use, insufficient for the claim’s route, or based on a non-admitted check version.

The biology does not map one-to-one onto evidence, applicability, and admission. The transfer preserves the need for multiple typed questions, not a fixed sequence or universal reviewer role.

#### I-T4 — No positive selection means non-authority, not fabricated support `[fit: solid]`

The donor’s default apoptosis cannot be copied literally because Trust preserves proposals and failures as history. The transferable rule is narrower: absence of an authorized admission or adequate applicable evidence leaves an item proposed, unresolved, rejected, or revoked. It cannot become active through mere persistence, popularity, or the lack of an objection.

#### I-T5 — Challenge and repair can be cyclic without erasing lineage `[fit: plausible]`

Germinal-center cells may undergo repeated mutation, testing, selection, and re-entry. Trust similarly needs versioned proposal, check, challenge, replay, repair, reassessment, and possible revision cycles. Each cycle must retain its law, case, context, causality, and lineage; a later pass cannot retroactively change the earlier observation.

The donor does not justify endless automated mutation of domain rules. Every new semantic version still crosses the admission boundary.

#### I-T6 — Competitive helper scarcity is not a general Trust rule `[fit: reach]`

Tfh help is limiting and comparative, making population selection possible. A Trust project may use reviewers, code owners, signatures, manifests, or another project-owned admission mechanism, but the donor does not justify mandatory independent reviewers, approval quotas, winner-take-all claims, or resource competition as semantics of trust.

The mechanism is retained only as a warning against letting the candidate and evaluator be the same unbounded authority.

#### I-T7 — Output feedback must not self-adjust truth requirements `[fit: reach]`

Secreted antibody can alter the shared antigen environment and raise selection pressure. Trust evidence and usage may reveal that a policy or domain rule deserves revision, but neither can automatically rewrite the required claims, evidence adequacy, or admission rules. A model may propose such a revision; domain or consumer authority decides it.

### Boundary and negative control

Classical T-independent type-2 responses are a nearby immune mechanism in which repetitive antigens strongly cross-link B-cell receptors and drive rapid, often extrafollicular responses without the cognate peptide–MHC-II → Tfh → CD40L selection gate. They can still produce class switching and memory-like outputs, so output shape alone does not prove the helper-gated mechanism occurred. [I18](#i18) [I19](#i19) [I20](#i20)

This negative case prevents a false universalization. Trust may permit an authorized, scoped, expiring consumer-policy override that lets an endpoint deploy before full evidence is renewed. That is a fast path around a consumer requirement, not admission of a new domain rule, proof that missing evidence exists, or conversion of a rapid result into durable semantic authority. If a fast path returns an output that resembles the fully gated path, the provenance and authority route must still remain distinguishable.

## Mechanisms that should not transfer

1. **Pheromone popularity as confidence.** Traffic and repeated traces can coordinate attention, but counts, links, task activity, model agreement, or repeated assertions cannot support a claim.
2. **One global decay constant.** Evidence freshness depends on domain-owned context and dependencies. Clocks apply only where a package or policy explicitly defines them.
3. **Unqualified “searched,” “passed,” or “complete.”** Every such state must retain method, scope, depth, omissions, reach, and the authority that interprets it.
4. **One universal marking vocabulary.** FEMA and INSARAG use different systems under an incident authority. Trust can standardize mechanics and stable condition envelopes while domain packages own meaning and presentation.
5. **A universal immune governance topology.** The helper gate supports separate proposal and admission, not mandatory code-owner independence, competitive approval scarcity, or deletion of rejected proposals.
6. **Survival or usage as truth.** A long-lived cell, popular trace, persistent issue, shipped endpoint, or merged PR may reveal selection pressure; none is evidence merely because it persisted.
7. **One substrate for commands and memory.** Site marks and pheromone trails are asynchronous memory. Radio dispatch and tandem running show that live invocation, delivery, acknowledgment, cancellation, and failure need different semantics.
8. **Exceptions that confer semantic authority.** Rapid clearance and T-independent activation show legitimate bounded bypasses in their domains. In Trust, a policy override can alter a consumer action while the underlying evidence condition and admission state remain visible and unchanged.

## Negative-control result

- **Tandem running:** transfer fails for synchronous dyadic interaction because there is no durable environmental trace, anonymous aggregation, reinforcement, or evaporation. The ant mapping survives only for shared asynchronous condition/work state.
- **Live radio dispatch:** transfer fails for commands because acknowledged, prioritized, capacity-constrained delivery cannot be replaced by a discoverable mark. The US&R mapping survives for durable records and local orientation.
- **T-independent B-cell activation:** transfer fails as a model of helper-gated admission because similar outputs can arise without that gate. The immunology mapping survives only as separation of generation, external evaluation, admission, retention, and provenance.

Together, the controls reject a universal “everything is a trace” model. Trace coordination, transactional commands, evidentiary records, semantic admission, and consumer policy remain separate kinds of relation.
