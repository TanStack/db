# Stage 5 outline-family map: TanStack Trust

## Status and boundary

This is the family pass of the Editorial Design Assay. It compares three
unranked organizing logics for the validated `TT-RFC-BAC` candidate. It does
not contain RFC section headings, select a family, repair theory, add research,
or approve an outline.

The three options were produced in one informed orchestrator context. They are
therefore correlated even though their organizing rules differ.

## Plain presentation baseline

A proposed shared software-evidence component lets project-owned packages
define guarantees, rules, checks, and relevant contexts. It records
execution-linked observations, evaluates declared combinations of required
support, retains contradictions and bounded repair history, and reports current
support without deciding whether a consumer may act. Documented procedures help
people and agents design, establish, maintain, and repair evidence; command-line,
editor, and agent adapters call one local service. A bounded prototype and one
Endpoints package instantiate only part of this design. Generic package
authoring, richer applicability, demonstrated workflow effectiveness, installed
clients, independent range, and production operation remain open.

## Reader movement and fixed design controls

The intended reader movement is from several dense, status-mixed artifacts to
an evaluable model of who owns each decision, how evidence gains and loses
authority, what agents can actually operate today, why conservative rules
exist, and which questions remain unsettled.

Every family must preserve:

- the use-case narrative as the high-level spine;
- an orthogonal architecture, semantic, and status reference plane;
- separate evidence, execution/reach, and consumer-policy axes;
- point-of-use distinctions among proposed, normative, implemented, bounded,
  absent, and unmeasured material;
- a CLI/LSP/MCP semantic-capability matrix that exposes missing generic
  authoring, registration, argument, and ingestion operations;
- applicability as a visible divergence between the grammar and current kernel;
- replay causality as a bounded prototype rule that still trusts checker
  freshness and does not cover every repair disposition;
- Endpoints as a worked consumer explicitly marked as not range evidence; and
- a compact account of borrowed mechanisms and their breakpoints, never donor
  success as evidence.

## Source-supported design inventory

The available source shapes are the evolutionary record, the six-primitive
semantic grammar, the current-support mechanism chain, parallel ownership
layers, the Endpoints worked-example cluster, and the audit-and-repair loop. No
one shape subsumes the others. Supported devices include an authority chain, a
state-axis table, a semantic-capability matrix, status labels, counterexample
witnesses, an unresolved-decision register, and the donor/breakpoint ledger.

The available examples are bounded: AND/OR route flattening; fingerprint
`a → b → a`; stale, late-delivered, and same-batch replay; expired resolution
authority; operational non-reach; and the Endpoints pass/fail/unresolved cases.

## Family F1 — Continuous claim journey

### Organizing logic

Follow one generic claim package through the complete operating sequence:
design, evidence establishment, inspection, freshness, contradiction, repair,
agent access, and consumer handoff. The Endpoints material appears as a bounded
sidecar at the relevant moments rather than as the claim that carries the
general narrative.

### Load-bearing source shape

The mechanism chain and evolutionary sequence carry the movement. Ownership
layers and source status appear as a stable reference plane that is revisited
whenever authority changes hands.

### Intended reader movement

The reader learns the system by watching one guarantee change state. By the end,
they can replay the lifecycle and identify the owner, evidence status, and
public operation at each transition.

### Foregrounds

- End-to-end causality and continuity.
- The distinction between recording an observation and granting current
  support.
- Why freshness, retained contradiction, and exact replay exist.
- The moment consumer permission leaves the base.

### Strands or compresses

- Independent lookup by task becomes less convenient.
- Claims with no check and alternate evidence forms receive less natural space.
- The interface capability matrix and unresolved-decision register can feel
  like interruptions to the journey.

### Borrowed mechanisms and breakpoints

Use one compact lineage interlude after the plain system baseline, then refer
back to it only where a borrowing materially shaped a rule. Lean supplies the
small-base separation; Field Lab supplies workflow ancestry; shadcn/lint and
Beads supply unimplemented interface/evaluation ideas; Endpoints supplies a
bounded consumer. Each entry states where the transfer stops.

### Expected-basin exposure and chief risk

This family sits closest to the expected lifecycle/workflow groove. Its chief
risk is becoming a polished product tour in which each verb turns into a
feature, Endpoints quietly becomes the general package, and status caveats arrive
too late. The fixed reference plane and point-of-use capability labels are
load-bearing controls.

### Reconstruction check

The validated remainder survives if the generic claim—not Endpoints—carries the
journey, the status plane remains visible at every transition, and the full
authority and interface gaps remain reconstructible outside the chronology.

## Family F2 — Modular use-case casebook

### Organizing logic

Treat each recurring job as a self-contained case: what the person or agent is
trying to do, what the base can know, what authority is required, which public
operation exists, what can go wrong, and what remains open. Shared semantic and
status maps provide the common contract among cases.

### Load-bearing source shape

The parallel ownership layers, task/work projections, public interface losses,
and Oracle portfolio supply the form. The mechanism chain appears locally
inside each case rather than as one continuous narrative.

### Intended reader movement

The reader can enter through the problem they are currently solving and leave
with both the immediate procedure and its place in the larger authority model.
The result behaves as an evaluable operating reference rather than a single
story.

### Foregrounds

- Human and agent tasks, especially what is possible now versus only proposed.
- CLI/LSP/MCP semantic operability and missing generic operations.
- The three independent axes of evidence, execution, and consumer policy.
- The distinction between an unresolved requirement and an implementation task.

### Strands or compresses

- The causal continuity of freshness, contradiction, and repair is fragmented
  across cases.
- Repeated status and authority fields can become mechanical.
- The repair history may look like a set of cautions rather than the reason the
  model changed.

### Borrowed mechanisms and breakpoints

Keep one compact lineage ledger near the shared contract, then link each donor
to the cases it influenced. This makes transfer limits easy to inspect but risks
making the donors look like a feature-source catalog; the ledger must retain
their non-evidentiary status.

### Expected-basin exposure and chief risk

This family overlaps the workflow and interface basins. Its chief risk is
normalizing into documentation for an assurance API: a collection of commands,
schemas, and lifecycle operations whose shared semantic mechanism and repair
causality disappear. A common claim/argument/challenge model and a cross-case
mechanism trace are required controls.

### Reconstruction check

The validated remainder survives if the cases remain applications of one
semantic and authority model, every unavailable operation is explicit, and a
reader can reconstruct the complete support-and-repair sequence across them.

## Family F3 — Braided work, authority, and repair

### Organizing logic

Move through the same ordered use cases on three synchronized rails: what the
human or agent does; what the base records, infers, or refuses to infer; and
which failure witness or repair constraint explains the boundary. A persistent
status band identifies proposed, normative, implemented, absent, and unmeasured
material.

### Load-bearing source shape

This family directly combines the workflow sequence, parallel ownership model,
and audit-and-repair loop without allowing any one to substitute for the others.
The semantic grammar is the shared cross-section through all three rails.

### Intended reader movement

The reader learns to inspect an operation and its authority simultaneously.
They see not only how to perform the work, but why the base may or may not accept
its result and which concrete failure made the boundary necessary.

### Foregrounds

- The selected hierarchy: use cases lead while architecture and repair explain
  each transition.
- Point-of-use status, trust roots, and authority handoffs.
- The difference between a workflow instruction, a kernel guarantee, a public
  operation, and an unmeasured outcome.
- The source-specific repair record that generic assurance-service language
  tends to erase.

### Strands or compresses

- The simultaneous rails increase cognitive load and can slow an introductory
  reading.
- The broad architecture can overshadow everyday task ergonomics.
- Repeated cross-references may make the RFC feel like an annotated design
  ledger rather than a clean narrative.

### Borrowed mechanisms and breakpoints

Use a compact fourth lineage band at the moment each borrowed mechanism first
appears, followed by one consolidated breakpoint ledger. This best preserves
translation context, but it risks giving donors too much visual authority; the
band must remain explicitly historical and non-validating.

### Expected-basin exposure and chief risk

This family is furthest from a simple product tour but closest to a dense
architecture document. Its chief risk is excess cognitive load: the use-case
spine can become nominal while the rails turn architecture and provenance into
the real subject. Each passage must still advance the reader's current task.

### Reconstruction check

The validated remainder survives if the use-case sequence remains the only
forward motion, the other rails explain rather than redirect it, and the
lineage band never carries an inference that target-domain sources cannot
support.

## Shared misfit register

No family can honestly absorb these gaps as solved material:

- ownership and cardinality of applicability;
- generic package extension and public authoring operations;
- rule, checker, analyzer, and dependency-capture soundness;
- alternate repair dispositions and cross-version mapping;
- demonstrated human or coding-agent workflow effectiveness;
- independent range beyond the Endpoints-shaped lineage;
- installed clients, distribution, multi-process durability, hostile-process
  integrity, scale, and production readiness; or
- an external novelty claim.

These remain visible constraints or return questions. Presentation cannot
repair them.

## Family-choice checkpoint

The next step requires one explicit choice: select `F1`, `F2`, or `F3`; combine
specific named properties while preserving one dominant organizing rule;
request a bounded revision of the family map; reject all three; or stop. No
concrete outline should be drafted before that choice.
