## Candidate admissions and rejections

### Admitted as primitives

Claim, observation, rule, argument, applicability condition, and challenge
survive as the six reusable evidence primitives. Each reconstructs a distinct
relation and changes a legal operation.

### Kept as non-primitive model units

- Run: shared observation provenance.
- Task: projection of an unresolved route gap.
- Evidence route: structured relation among claims, rules, observations, and
  arguments.
- Dependency revision: currentness mechanism.
- Workflow and interface: carriers.
- Consumer policy: separate semantic owner.
- Status: claim about maturity or evidence, not evidence itself.

### Rejected or merged

| Candidate | Treatment | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trust package | Rejected | The source supports an internal Endpoints layer, not a present package boundary. |
| Trust score | Rejected | It collapses evidence, reach, applicability, and policy. |
| Confidence primitive | Rejected | No universal confidence semantics are selected. |
| Formal solver | Rejected | Solver choice and encoding remain domain-owned evidence forms. |
| Currentness, validity, and fresh authority as three units | Merged | Applicability, dependency revision, and current repair authority reconstruct the distinctions with less duplication. |
| Green result | Rejected as a semantic unit | Outcome color does not establish route support, reach, or permission. |
| Workflow completion | Rejected as evidence | Following steps does not grant authority. |
| Interface result | Rejected as evidence | Transport does not change semantics. |
| Separate Endpoints and Trust products | Rejected | Authority separation operates inside one product for the foreseeable future. |

## Process risk

The extraction turns prose into a precise graph and may overstate how modular
or settled the design is. The overlap ledger, unresolved-priority register, and
decomposition-loss section counter that tendency but cannot eliminate it. The
dependency graph is analyst-inferred from one RFC, not empirical evidence of
the best teaching order or build plan.
