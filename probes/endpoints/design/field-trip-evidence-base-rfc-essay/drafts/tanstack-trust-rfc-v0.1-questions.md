# TanStack Trust RFC v0.1 — question and gap register

## Load-bearing unsupported additions

None. Every load-bearing gap encountered during drafting was kept visible as an
unresolved decision, an absent operation, an unmeasured outcome, or a bounded
claim in the RFC.

## Questions the draft returns rather than answers

1. Who owns applicability, and is its result boolean or three-valued?
2. What is the stable public domain-package and registration contract?
3. What governance lets an agent propose a rule without authorizing its own
   semantic trust root?
4. What explanation schema preserves complete routes without creating
   unbounded or unusable output?
5. How should exact claims and counterexamples map across versions, scopes, and
   alternate repairs?
6. Which consumer policies should the first Endpoints integration exercise?
7. What second, independently developed domain is suitable for a real range
   test?
8. Which human/agent study can test workflow and diagnostic effectiveness
   without recycling the system's own checks as the success criterion?

## Stranded source material

- The full historical evolution of the 6,337-line source Field Log is compressed
  into design lineage rather than narrated chronologically.
- The Oracle Guide's detailed portfolio procedures are represented through the
  three workflow summaries; its complete card remains a separate source.
- Formal-system source particulars are condensed into the donor ledger so they
  do not make formal checks appear preferred over empirical or rubric-assessed
  routes.
- The thirteen original Stage 3 candidate bodies remain preserved but do not
  appear as alternative RFCs after Kyle selected the composite TanStack Trust
  candidate.
