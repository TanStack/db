## Compression and ablation result

- All 82 concepts survive because each reconstructs at least one sourced
  distinction, constrains a legal transformation, or preserves a named
  property or overlap.
- The original candidate “six base primitives” was not allowed to absorb runs,
  tasks, evidence axes, workflows, interfaces, status labels, or policy. Those
  remain relations, projections, carriers, or state distinctions rather than
  extra primitives.
- The original candidate “Trust package” was rejected. The source supports an
  internal architectural layer inside Endpoints, not a present distribution
  unit.
- “Confidence,” “strength,” “priority,” and “trust score” were rejected as
  generic base concepts because the RFC explicitly leaves them unselected.
- “Formal solver” was rejected as a base primitive. Formal systems contribute
  domain-owned evidence and explanation forms.
- O01–O12 survive overlap ablation: removing any one loses a handoff or
  cross-layer condition used by the source.
- I01–I18 and R01–R16 survive rule ablation. The detailed item-by-item effects
  are recorded in DG-PROC1.

The minimal-description preference removed duplicate concept candidates for
“currentness,” “validity,” and “fresh authority.” They are represented by
C28, C40–C43, and C47–C48 rather than three parallel vocabularies.

## Range control

**Range status: untested.** No independent marginal case was supplied. The
Endpoints case was used to form and reconstruct the model, so it cannot test
whether the model transfers to other TanStack libraries or other software
domains. No range case was sought.

The hypothetical Query, Router, Form, or other TanStack examples requested for
the next draft may clarify the design. Until checked, they remain
illustrations, not marginal cases and not evidence of portability.

## Exclusion control

**Negative case N01:** a generic test dashboard stores only the latest red or
green result, lets one plugin define the check and deployment action, and
discards prior failures.

The frozen model rejects N01 because it violates:

- I02: semantic and action authority collapse into one plugin;
- I04: route shape is flattened;
- I06: evidence, reach, and policy collapse into one status;
- I08: currentness lacks historical dependency revision;
- I09 and I10: contradiction and repair history disappear; and
- I13: the latest green result upgrades the whole state.

The model therefore does not generate N01 as a legal simplification. This is a
model-derived exclusion, not an empirical comparison with a separately sourced
system.

- F01 changes the structure to one expanding case.

## Decomposition loss

Splitting the RFC into concepts loses four whole-text properties unless they
are restored during projection:

1. **The felt burden:** the individual mechanics only matter because people
   currently reconstruct, judge, and maintain guarantees by hand.
2. **The coupled product shape:** domain semantics, evidence mechanics, and
   policy are separate authorities but one Endpoints product.
3. **The temporal story:** freshness, contradiction, replay, and expiry make
   sense as a history, not as a static component list.
4. **The status rhythm:** the source repeatedly places implementation,
   proposal, absence, and uncertainty next to one another.

The layered brief must restore all four. A polished hierarchy is not allowed to
imply clean module independence, settled semantics, safe automation outcomes,
or portability.
