The specific status distinctions above have no counterpart in v0.3. In the same conversion, lines 766, 769, 772, 783, 786, and 790 generalize or omit the named donor details (`ATMS and structured argument systems`, `Datalog`, `SAT and SMT`, `Evidence-oriented workflows`, `Lint-style diagnostics`, and `Task systems`). The mechanisms and most prohibited inferences remain, but the source/inspection/negative-control/workflow-lineage status of those mechanisms does not.
- **Drop mechanism:** category mismatch.

### 2. The Datalog breakpoint no longer says that positive derivation fails to model applicability and defeat

- **Exact source passage:** Line 587: `Entailment is relative to supplied facts and rules; positive derivation does not model applicability, defeat, expiry, or repair`.

Applicability and defeat appear elsewhere in v0.3 as general architecture concerns, so the weakened point is specifically that a positive Datalog derivation does not itself model them.
- **Drop mechanism:** compression.

Neither passage retains the conditional idea that deciding to extract may itself create the reason and timing for a second-domain range test.

A plugin is one hostile-process category; the source's broader process boundary is not retained.
- **Drop mechanism:** category mismatch.
