# Frozen Stage 3 source-grounded candidate map

## Freeze and controls

This map was frozen after three fresh, sibling-hidden source generators completed and before any blind expectation probe was dispatched or read. All three generators received the same complete typed handoff and only their own coordinate assignment. Their exact traces are:

- [`stage-3-source-system.md`](stage-3-source-system.md)
- [`stage-3-source-use.md`](stage-3-source-use.md)
- [`stage-3-source-limits.md`](stage-3-source-limits.md)

Eighteen accepted generator cards collapsed to thirteen semantically distinct candidates. Merge means the public question, intervention, form, and source basis were materially the same; it is not a ranking or validation result. Every candidate below remains unvalidated and unranked. IDs preserve this frozen map order only.

## `RFC-C01` — The system as coupled jurisdictions

- **Public question:** How do base mechanics, claim packages, authoring workflows, consumer policy, and transport adapters cooperate without borrowing one another's authority?
- **Upstream support:** `BR-02/03`; `ESS-S01` (Field Log 3875–3887; grammar README 9–17, 32–50; `model.json:P1/R1/R9`; workflows README 13–28; prototype README 9–16, 134–154); `ESS-S02` (grammar README 14–21; `model.json:C1–C6`; `process.json:A-run/A-task/A-score/A-package/A-policy`; `kernel.ts:17–97`); `ESS-S09`; `ESS-S10`; `ESS-TOPO-04`; recovered authority items `LFG-H12` (`FL:2731–2756`) and `LGI-V1-12` (`model.json:P6`; `source.md:P20/P21/P29/P32/L7`).
- **Control status:** seams are allowed support; `ESS-TOPO-04` is descriptive; the two authority items remain recoveries, not required architecture.
- **Intervention:** replace a component inventory with an authority-preserving system map showing what each layer may assert, inspect, package, transport, or decide.
- **Reader-promise hypothesis:** Kyle can place a proposed change in the correct ownership layer and see the neighboring contracts it affects.
- **Form and scale:** full RFC; end-to-end claim-to-consumer trace plus a layer/authority matrix.
- **Gaps:** `ESS-T01/T03/T04/T09`; `ESS-E01/E03/E10/E13`.
- **Source status:** confirmed brief, provisional architecture, bounded implementation, and supported recovered omissions; general product architecture remains untested.
- **Injected choices:** “coupled jurisdictions,” the matrix, and an end-to-end trace are editorial structure.
- **Risks:** boundaries may look more deployable or settled than they are; Endpoints may dominate; recoveries may look mandatory.
- **Related candidates:** broad parent of `RFC-C03`, `RFC-C04`, `RFC-C05`, and `RFC-C08`.
- **Reconstruction:** source generator `ECM-SM-01`; every component and boundary comes from the named inputs, with no new authority.
