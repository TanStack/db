## Overlay-induced source re-scan

One additional supported distinction became salient only after comparing the
expected evidence-to-policy basin with the body:

- **Atlas-induced residual — fallback is policy, not a universal safe state.**
  Section 9 says unresolved evidence is neither a universal veto nor
  permission, and that a full refetch may itself be unsafe while effects remain
  in flight or external systems are unobserved. The public frame makes policy
  ownership clear but does not preview this stronger refusal of a built-in
  “conservative” fallback.

This is an RFC source claim, not a new theory or a reason to change the opening.

## Coverage holes and borders

- The public frame carries the whole-system handoff and broad temporal story.
  It does not test whether readers recover exact evidence, freshness, and repair
  rules from the body. The scheduled Reader Assay owns that question.
- BC3-01's separation-leak concern marks a border around applicability and rule
  soundness. The body may show whether the architecture assigns that judgment
  to domains while still offering reusable traversal and history.
- The word “Trust” can still invite proof, certification, safety approval, or a
  standalone product. All three probes also recovered the explicit refusals,
  so this sample shows tension rather than full collapse.
- Donor breakpoints, detailed status vocabulary, admission governance,
  operational-failure history, and service/security omissions remain invisible
  at public-frame depth.

Unsampled cells include human maintainers, different model families,
title-only interpretation, non-English reading, body-level recovery, actual
agent use, and product outcomes. No adaptive border sample was run.

## Main distortion

The abstract and section sequence state the intended architecture unusually
explicitly. That can make the three probes converge more than a shorter public
description would. Shared model lineage further correlates the outputs. The
overlay therefore shows which distinctions this framing makes easy or hard to
reconstruct; it does not validate the architecture or predict audience
response.
