# Stage 5 O2 blind-outline overlay

## Sample

Three fresh sibling-hidden probes received the complete P2 public specimen and
one of three meaning-preserving prompt variants: canonical (`BC-01`),
owner-first (`BC-02`), or neutral substantive reconstruction (`BC-03`). The
source track in `stage-5-o2-blind-outline-plan.md` was frozen before their
outputs were read. No adaptive sample was added.

The samples share one model family. Exact recurrence below describes only this
setup; it is not public opinion, probability, novelty, or quality.

## Expected basins

### B1 — Domain-specific evidence base (`3/3`, all prompt variants)

Every probe expected a reusable base or infrastructure layer embedded beneath
domain-specific guarantee systems. None reconstructed an overarching certifier.

### B2 — Five-part ownership handoff (`3/3`, all prompt variants)

Every probe reconstructed domain packages as owners of meaning and checks, the
base as owner of evidence mechanics, workflows as maintainers, interfaces as
exposure, and consumers as owners of permission and fallback.

### B3 — Evidence maintenance lifecycle (`3/3`, all prompt variants)

Every probe expected support, contradiction, freshness or aging, and repair to
form the central mechanism. The mechanism was described as inspectable records
or bookkeeping rather than proof of domain truth.

### B4 — Proposed and incomplete system (`3/3`, all prompt variants)

Every probe classified the RFC as a proposal with unfinished or undecided work,
not a complete production system.

### B5 — Endpoints as a bounded demonstration and generalization risk (`3/3`, all prompt variants)

Every probe treated Endpoints as the concrete bounded vertical. Every probe also
identified the move from that one case to a general base as the largest or a
major overclaim risk.

### B6 — Generic donor and decision ledger (`3/3`, all prompt variants)

Every probe expected borrowed evidence, provenance, verification, or maintenance
mechanisms plus explicit breakpoints and open decisions. None could reconstruct
the named donor systems from the public elements.

## Useful overlap and collapse boundary

The packaging reliably communicates the product shape and authority chain. It
also causes fresh readers to locate the hardest unresolved seam without seeing
the source map: BC-01 warns that generic support/freshness/repair semantics could
determine domain truth; BC-03 asks whether those statuses themselves require
domain meaning. This overlaps the source constraint that the base may evaluate
only domain-declared routes and applicability. The RFC must show that split at
the mechanism, not merely state it in the authority map.

No atlas-induced residual was added: the seam was already present in SM2, SM3,
and SM8 before overlay.

## Main source-specific losses

The public outline reconstructs the **why** and **who** more readily than the
precise **how** and **limits**. Likely collapse paths are:

- explicit AND/OR arguments become generic evidence bookkeeping;
- operational non-reach disappears into missing or negative evidence;
- exact replay causality becomes ordinary repair or re-testing;
- applicability becomes an assumed property of freshness;
- formal witnesses become unnamed verification mechanisms;
- interface parity becomes a generic shared service; and
- unresolved generality becomes a conventional implementation backlog.

## Border regions and remaining holes

Another sample could change the map around title-only interpretation, the word
“guarantees,” and whether a different model family invents a universal solver or
certification layer. Those cells were not sampled here. Human maintainer
reconstruction, non-English reading, prose-level recovery, and real agent
authoring also remain open.

The scheduled quick checkpoint stops after the initial three-probe family. The
expected basins are stable across all three prompt forms, and the remaining
holes require a different stratum rather than another adaptive replicate of the
same setup.

## Control limits

- Shared model lineage means the three outputs are correlated.
- The description and headings explicitly encode the ownership map, so its
  recurrence does not establish that prose will preserve it.
- Model-default reconstruction cannot establish public prevalence or reader
  value.
- Endpoints generalization is a predicted reader risk, not an observed human
  misunderstanding.
