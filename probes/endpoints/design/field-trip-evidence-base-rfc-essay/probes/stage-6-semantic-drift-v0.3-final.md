## Meaning invariants

The final draft must continue to establish all of the following without the
snapshot:

1. Trust is an internal Endpoints architecture, with no standalone product or
   current second-domain plan.
2. Domain packages own meaning and checks; Trust owns evidence history and
   assessment; consumer policy owns action.
3. Implemented, bounded, proposed, normative, absent, unmeasured, unresolved,
   and unselected claims remain distinguishable.
4. AND/OR routes, execution reach, applicability, historical freshness,
   contradiction, causal same-case repair, and expiry keep their meaning.
5. Recovered v0.2 material keeps its original support and status and does not
   become new theory.
6. Router, Query, Table, and Form cases remain hypothetical and do not become
   range evidence.
