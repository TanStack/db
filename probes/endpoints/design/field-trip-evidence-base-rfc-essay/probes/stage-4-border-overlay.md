## Recurring expected structure

The following moves recurred in all three probes and all three wording/coordinate variants:

1. A chronological use-case route from claim/check definition through evidence, current support, freshness, contradiction, repair, interfaces, and policy handoff (`3/3`).
2. Architecture and authority introduced as supporting explanations at workflow boundaries rather than as the opening spine (`3/3`).
3. CLI, LSP, and MCP treated as projections of one underlying trust/evidence model (`3/3`).
4. Consumer-specific severity and permission kept downstream of evidence assessment (`3/3`).
5. A bounded prototype plus one worked consumer used to illustrate rather than define the general layer (`3/3`).

This is a **deep expected groove** under the sampled prompt family. It says the composition is readily reconstructible; it does not establish quality, truth, novelty, or source support.

## Recurring collapse basin

All three probes described a closely related generic collapse (`3/3`):

- a feature-oriented assurance/provenance platform;
- a conventional evidence or verification control-plane RFC; or
- a metadata registry with lifecycle states, persistence, and three adapters.

In that basin the verbs become separate feature sections, the architecture becomes a central service diagram, and the repair witnesses move into background or an appendix.

## Strand balance

- **Use-case spine:** survived in all three probes (`3/3`).
- **Architecture/authority:** survived as supporting material in all three (`3/3`), but the wording alone could not preserve whether boundaries are semantic, organizational, process-local, storage, protocol, or security boundaries.
- **Failure/repair rationale:** named in all three (`3/3`), but its causal content weakened in all three (`3/3`). The probes could not know whether a witness produced a rule, merely illustrated it later, or was observed versus constructed.

## Distinctions the public wording cannot preserve without sources

Across the three traces, the wording alone could not hold apart:

- claim, check, observation, evidence, and derived support;
- freshness, contradiction, check failure, staleness, and supersession;
- repair of software, evidence, rule, metadata, or presentation;
- producer authority, accepted control, runner observation, assessment, and consumer permission;
- one shared semantic service from merely similar transport commands;
- domain illustration from domain semantics required by the core; and
- observed prototype failures, constructed examples, historical causes, and retrospective illustrations.

These are source-dependent distinctions, not defects established by the blind probes.

## Concrete collapse scene

One claim is introduced, but each lifecycle verb becomes a separate feature chapter. The CLI/LSP/MCP sections then tour equivalent commands. A new green result replaces or visually dominates an earlier failure, while “repair” becomes metadata editing and severity becomes a configuration panel. A later appendix recounts the old failure, but no source-traced link shows that it caused the contradiction-retention rule. Every named ingredient remains present; the use-case, authority, and repair-rationale spines have collapsed into a generic product tour.

## Border result

The composite candidate's intended hierarchy is recoverable from its public wording, but the architecture and especially the failure/repair rationale require source-bound drafting to survive. The largest collapse risk is **feature/API/metadata-service normalization**. The remaining validation gates should test whether source trace and mechanism can prevent that collapse.

## Controls and limits

Three probes are correlated by shared model lineage. One probe per variant cannot estimate population frequency. The prompt itself names use cases, three interfaces, policy separation, a prototype, and a worked consumer, so recurrence of those ingredients is partly induced. No alternate model family, exact-prompt replicate, source-aware reader, or secondary audience was sampled. The reading is non-binding for eligibility.
