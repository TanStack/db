## State order

1. A named established disqualifier → `ineligible under a named gate`.
2. Otherwise an unresolved theory or disputed interpretation → `returned for theory or disputed interpretation`.
3. Otherwise incomplete or inconclusive evidence-bearing work → `held for named evidence or research`.
4. Otherwise a complete candidate that cannot carry the promised standalone RFC → `orientation only under this brief`.
5. Otherwise → `eligible for user choice`.

Blind recurrence never clears or fails the candidate. Null clears only the named absence tested. Missing input is never evidence of absence. Conflicting decision-bearing readings remain held or returned; agreement counts and model confidence cannot settle them.
