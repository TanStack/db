# Stage 4 candidate-border Blind Cartography plan

## Freeze

This plan was frozen before any candidate-border probe was dispatched or read. Each probe sees only the frozen public candidate, reader promise, and its assigned wording/coordinate. It sees no source corpus, Stage 3 candidates, atlas, author preference, eligibility policy, or sibling output.

## Frozen public candidate

> TanStack Trust is a proposed reusable evidence layer for making software guarantees inspectable and maintainable by humans and agents. The RFC uses concrete work as its high-level narrative: design a claim and check, establish evidence, inspect current support, maintain freshness, handle contradiction and repair, use the result through CLI/LSP/MCP, then hand off severity and permission to consumer policy. Architecture and authority appear where the work crosses system boundaries; concrete failure and repair witnesses explain why important rules exist. The current implementation is a bounded prototype, and one domain package is only a worked consumer.

## Fixed output contract

Return one source-free expectedness reading:

1. the argument or outline the wording most readily suggests;
2. the standard RFC moves or assumptions it invites;
3. which of use-case spine, architecture/authority, and failure/repair rationale dominates or disappears;
4. the nearest generic account it collapses toward;
5. distinctions the wording alone cannot preserve without source evidence; and
6. one concrete collapse-risk scene.

Do not rank, recommend, claim novelty, infer source support, or decide eligibility.

## Meaning-preserving variants and coordinates

- `CB-01`, direct wording, **use-case dominance** coordinate: test whether the operational spine remains primary or becomes a feature tour.
- `CB-02`, reordered wording, **architecture integration** coordinate: test whether authority boundaries support the use cases or take over as a conventional architecture RFC.
- `CB-03`, question-led wording, **repair-rationale integration** coordinate: test whether failure witnesses explain rules or become a detached history/case-study.

## Stop and limits

Three fresh probes satisfy the initial border reading. Shared model lineage remains correlation. No exact-prompt replicate, alternate model family, secondary reader, or source-aware reader is included. Another sample is justified only if the three probes disagree about which spine survives.
