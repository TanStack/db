# Evidence-system Structural Recombine

Date: 2026-09-15. Status: bounded pass, corrected after procedure audit. Selected by Kyle: “yup, very good, ok now structural recombine”.

This pass examined **eight candidate cross-links**. After checking them against the earlier lifecycle, **five remain candidate recombinations and three are retained only as confirmations**. Three unranked arrangements show ways to inspect those relations. It selects no architecture, storage format or check-level policy. No application or evidence-engine code was changed.

## Specimen and controls

The specimen is the four Process Grammars, reviewed decisions, two frames, prior boundary cases, RFC, oracle guide, authority challenge, Beads reading and new evidence-donor survey. The old lifecycle draft is a comparison target: merely rebuilding its sequence does not count as a new recombination.

Source pointers below identify the selected passages. [Source captures](structural-recombine-sources.json) retain file hashes, section locations and excerpts. External donor claims come through the inspected survey, with its original links and limits; no new literature search ran. The process cases remain bounded historical readings, not newly executed tests.

The analyst chose the parts. “Part” means a separately usable statement, operation or constraint, not an indivisible fact or a proposed database entity. Roles in the table are descriptive; they do not establish an evidence taxonomy. Original source order is removed, while each part keeps a reverse pointer.

Calibration:

- **solid:** the narrow joined relation already has a direct project requirement or recorded witness. This does not certify an implementation.
- **plausible:** the parts fit and survive the stated boundary contrast, but the new arrangement has not been tested.
- **reach:** the connection needs an additional unsupported premise. Retain it as a question, not an implementation requirement.

## Cross-links, tested one at a time

### L1 — Alternative support routes inside a conjunctive requirement

**Parts:** P01, P03, P04, P07, P18, P19, P34. **Operation:** distinguish conjunction between required claims from alternatives within one claim. **[fit: plausible]**

For a bounded selective-refresh case, a report could show separate obligations for a complete relevant effect bound, an applicable completion boundary, and correct reconciliation. A work-reduction claim adds its own observation. Static analysis and an accepted scoped investigation could be alternative support routes for one obligation; they are not substitutes for the other obligations.

This list is illustrative, not a sufficient global protocol proof. Ordinary framework obligations and the case's baseline conditions remain in force.

**Boundary contrast:** PG4's value-only success cannot establish reduced work. G2's complete write set cannot establish completion. Conversely, G1's coarse complete bound need not wait for exact conditional effects.

**Reverse check:** remove the joins and recover ATMS's alternative premise sets, PG4's distinct measurements, and G1/G2's distinct facts. None of those sources supplies the complete Endpoints support rule. The added structure is the placement of alternatives *inside* named conjunctive obligations, rather than a flat bag of evidence.

### L2 — Applicability belongs to a use of evidence

**Audit disposition: confirmation/refinement, excluded from the recombination count.** The earlier lifecycle already contains the central relation. Donors and cases clarify it but do not establish a new arrangement by themselves.

**Parts:** P02, P05, P09–P11, P14, P15, P19, P23, P24. **Operation:** match one observation to a particular claim, consumer and current context. **[fit: plausible]**

An observation could support several bounded claims with different assessments. Its run record remains fixed while each use has a reason it currently applies, fails to apply or leaves a premise open. Subject, method, environment and law are separate matching conditions.

**Boundary contrast:** the same no-PG-write finding may support refresh selection while leaving retry safety unanswered. A stubbed test retains its local claim; it cannot silently acquire provider scope. A rubric change can reassess an applicable record, while restored code still requires a fresh check under R22.

**Reverse check:** this retains the donors' subject/context descriptions without treating them as a sufficient fingerprint. It retains R19 and R22 as different transitions. Use-specific applicability confirms the existing lifecycle relation; it does not decide how to discover every dependency or admit sampled evidence.

### L3 — A claim's open premises generate shared work

**Parts:** P06, P11, P12, P17–P19, P27, P30, P31, P33. **Operation:** project missing premises from L1/L2 into prerequisite-aware investigations. **[fit: plausible]**

One unresolved shared-helper claim could produce one investigation linked to all compatible callers. The explanation would say which premise is missing, which alternative methods could settle it, what each method would leave open, and what downstream work it enables. Automatically derived facts would satisfy their premises without creating agent tasks.

**Boundary contrast:** discovering ten callers does not create ten copies of the task. Learning a partial footprint does not clear its completion question. If the helper actually returns before writes finish, the next work may require authored behavior rather than another certificate. An inconclusive note does not close the task.

**Reverse check:** Beads contributes query/interaction patterns, the review supplies sharing and priority, and the frames supply the knowledge/behavior distinction. None alone supplies this dependency projection. “Choose the cheapest investigation set” is a **[fit: reach]** extension: costs, success rates and complete dependencies are unavailable, so it is not retained as a scheduling rule.

### L4 — A contradiction challenges a particular use and leaves a history

**Parts:** P02, P13, P20–P22, P25, P28. **Operation:** join a directly reported counterexample to the claims/uses it contradicts, retaining event and discovery history. **[fit: plausible]**; direct reporting and original-failure rerun are **[fit: solid]** project requirements.

An arriving mismatch would record what law and observation failed, what subject/context was exercised, and which previously relied-on conclusion it challenges. A later result can explain repair, narrower scope or a different strategy without rewriting the earlier observation. Arrival order alone does not establish resolution.

**Boundary contrast:** a large new green campaign cannot erase a compatible unresolved counterexample merely by being newer. A reduction that changes a value mismatch into a read-count failure is not a replay of the original failure. Conversely, a report for an old version is not automatically a counterexample to unrelated current code; uncertain mapping remains explicit.

**Reverse check:** ATMS is not being used to erase observations or to deny all alternative support. Assurance challenges retain their target; R6/R7 determine the concrete response to a relevant oracle contradiction. R8 limits the effect to subsequent executions/deployments, with no claimed repair of open clients. Exact ingestion and affected-use discovery remain undesigned.

### L5 — Provenance can expose shared blind spots without assigning confidence

**Parts:** P02, P19, P20, P22, P26, P28. **Operation:** traverse shared semantic dependencies behind apparently different support routes. **[fit: plausible]**

Two green checks could share a comparator, fixture assumption or expected-value derivation. A report could name that common dependency and attach a challenge to the claimed independence. Source anchors make an inspection's actual premise reviewable; a fault control can address a specific blind spot.

**Boundary contrast:** ten seeds do not become ten independent models. Shared cleanup code alone does not establish a shared semantic blind spot. Separate file names also do not establish independent reasoning. An alternative formulation helps only for the faults its differing assumptions can expose.

**Reverse check:** PROV supplies the relation, the oracle guide supplies the semantic distinction, and grading/assurance supply reasons and challenges. The join does not calculate confidence or demand that every shared dependency be eliminated. Automatically discovering shared *reasoning*, rather than declared/code-level dependencies, remains **[fit: reach]**.

### L6 — One execution can produce several differently scoped observations

**Parts:** P02, P07–P09, P19–P21, P23. **Operation:** separate execution identity from the individual observations assessed against claims. **[fit: solid]** for the existing outcome distinctions; **[fit: plausible]** as a general record arrangement.

A single run can preserve a handler error, a passing committed-row assertion and a separate read-count result. An imported report can retain its producer and importer without changing those observations. Claim-specific assessment does not require duplicating the whole run for each conclusion.

**Boundary contrast:** PG3's failed application action does not make its test fail. PG4's value pass cannot lend credit to an absent latency measurement. PG2's aggregate test report does not manufacture missing provider or per-operation observations.

**Reverse check:** the research-object execution stays an execution, while each process case keeps its original observable and missing detail. This does not introduce evidence-log records for an environment that cannot run a check; R9 leaves that error in CI. Capture is not permission to store secrets or raw private inputs in the repository.

### L7 — Assessment authorship does not confer runner or policy authority

**Audit disposition: confirmation/refinement, excluded from the recombination count.** The earlier lifecycle already contains the central relation. Donors and cases clarify it but do not establish a new arrangement by themselves.

**Parts:** P16, P21, P24, P27, P29, P32. **Operation:** preserve provenance across record submission, assessment and permitted consumption. **[fit: solid]** for the separation; **[fit: plausible]** for its representation.

The same agent can inspect a helper and score that inspection, with both roles recorded. That does not let an edited record impersonate an automatic runner result, an accepted control change or an enforced consumer decision. Support status and project permission remain separately explainable.

**Boundary contrast:** a warning may coexist with enabled optimization under R15. It still does not establish the missing claim. An actual relevant oracle contradiction follows R6. A new comparator that sorts away promised order cannot inherit the old ordered-law assessment just because the runner genuinely passed.

**Reverse check:** nanopublications supply origin/publication separation; SLSA supplies expectations distinct from the artifact; the local authority challenge supplies enforcement questions. None selects signatures, a hosted service or mandatory separate review. R16's check-level mechanism remains deferred; this is not a hidden policy choice.

### L8 — A wider obligation survives a narrower supported result

**Audit disposition: confirmation/refinement, excluded from the recombination count.** The earlier lifecycle already contains the central relation. Donors and cases clarify it but do not establish a new arrangement by themselves.

**Parts:** P01, P02, P07, P09, P12, P17, P22, P30. **Operation:** attach bounded support to a requirement while retaining the uncovered remainder and its consequence. **[fit: plausible]**

For the optimization case, “read work reduced in these generated programs” can be supported while “latency improved in the intended deployment” remains unmeasured. For the helper, a source observation can settle one local question without clearing provider completion. The next action depends on whether the residue is missing evidence, missing behavior or an unspecified product limit.

**Boundary contrast:** renaming a broad claim to match a small observation must not erase the original requirement. But an unselected goal must not be invented: latency work is open only if that requirement actually exists. Unknown completion can deserve attention before known extra reads without being labeled a demonstrated failure.

**Reverse check:** the RFC preserves requirements, the oracle guide preserves open promises, the frames separate residual work, and assurance cases preserve doubts. This confirms an existing residue-to-work relation, not a new connection, score or rule that every uncertainty blocks execution.

## Three unranked arrangements

These are different ways of organizing the joined parts. They may overlap; they are not three mandatory services or a menu of product options. No winner or combined design is selected.

| Arrangement | What becomes central | Cross-links and concrete reading | What it hides if used alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Requirement with alternative support routes | A named obligation, its jointly required premises, and the alternative routes supporting each premise | L1/L2/L5/L8. A query can show a compiler-supported key rule beside an unresolved helper completion claim, with precise reasons for each. | Run history, storage mechanics and the authority of ingestion are not supplied by a support diagram. |
| B — Execution history with assessed uses | Captured observations, their origins and contexts, plus explanations of each current or historical use | L2/L4/L6/L7. A delayed counterexample can be compared with the use it challenges without overwriting a newer unrelated pass. | Starting only from existing runs misses requirements for which no check exists. A history is not itself a complete assessment. |
| C — Shared investigations derived from gaps | One task per unresolved shared claim, with prerequisites, alternative methods and affected endpoint views | L1/L3/L8. “Establish completion” can be shared across callers; a partial finding changes what remains to learn without automatically closing the task. | Task status can conceal partial support, provenance and actual consumer policy unless those remain separately accessible. |

These arrangements combine retained candidate links with confirmed existing relations. They carry no claim of general software novelty. An implementation could realize them with plain records and bounded rules; this pass supplies no reason to adopt a general theorem prover, event database or graph product.

## Remaining gaps and stop condition

The audit excludes L2, L7 and L8 from the novel-link count. L1, L3, L4, L5 and L6 retain narrower candidate operations described in the [procedure audit](structural-recombine-audit.md); their novelty and fit remain analyst judgments, not demonstrated benefits. This is the point to stop recombining: deciding legal support rules, record primitives and concrete interfaces belongs to the contemplated Design Grammar, not this result.

Open questions include claim equivalence across callers, conditional scope inclusion, dependency discovery, capture completeness, what a particular rubric accepts, affected-use matching for contradictions, and an inexpensive implementation. The priority model and exact check-level mechanism remain undecided. No measured agent reliability, trust, performance or maintenance benefit follows from this pass.

The decomposition itself favors graph-shaped arrangements: the corpus already discusses dependencies, and the analyst chose parts that expose joins. Arrangement B counters the tendency to treat every item as a logical premise; arrangement C counters the tendency to ignore work cost. Neither removes that selection bias. The same analyst produced the links and reverse checks, so these are not independent validation.

The original [v1 result](structural-recombine.v1.md) remains available. The [procedure audit](structural-recombine-audit.md) distinguishes original compliance from corrections made now. No production probes, new oracle campaign or formal evidence-system simulation ran. Validation covers captured-source integrity and local reference accounting only.
