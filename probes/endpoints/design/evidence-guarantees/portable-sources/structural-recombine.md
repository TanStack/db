# Structural recombinator (`structural-recombine`)

- **Phenomenon / range / input:** New supported cross-links among parts once source arrangements are removed; needs grounded positions and optional donors.
- **Why use it:** Whole arguments preserve their source structure. Decomposition makes parts and cross-source links available for separate fit tests.
- **Perturbation / procedure:** Decompose each source into traceable qualities, attributes, relations, operations, constraints, and promised outcomes. Remove its original argumentative order while retaining source pointers. Propose cross-source links one at a time, state what operation joins the parts, calibrate each link `[fit: solid|plausible|reach]`, and test whether reversing the decomposition reconstructs the source claims. Reject links that merely rebuild one source or rely on an unnamed taxonomy.
- **Readout / control:** Atomic parts, proposed links, fit calibration, and reversibility trace; reject same-arrangement rebuilds.
- **Common artifacts:** The analyst invents “atoms,” imports a hidden taxonomy, or treats reach connections as solid.
- **Escalate / stop:** Escalate when a new arrangement has emergent structure. Stop when recombinations only restate an input.
- **What it requires:** Deep analysis; offer a Field Log for a bounded map, or keep it in the round file and wiki during the dialectic workflow.
- **Execution placement:** **Orchestrator.** It needs all grounded parts, donor readings, calibration, and reversibility constraints to test cross-links. Fresh subagents may propose links from bounded part sets, but the orchestrator applies the fit controls. If the full source trace cannot fit, promote rather than delegate blindly. Return proposed arrangements, traceable links, and calibration without selecting or synthesizing them.
