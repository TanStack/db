## Layer reconstruction checklist

- Public bare-collection and synchronous-transaction constraints remain visible in the brief.
- Public activity signals are distinguished from unresolved preload/pin/readiness policy.
- Both optimistic and authoritative impact/patch stages are visible.
- Equivalent-query sharing is not presented as solving arbitrary overlapping queries.
- Storage and response strategy remain independent axes.
- Small delta derivation is not equated with avoiding server query execution.
- The insert/deletion contrast names its sufficient-data assumptions.
- The latency comparison includes inline full results; measured and assumed quantities remain distinct.
- Error availability, no hidden queue, scope and server-code boundary remain constraints.
- Unresolved write mapping, snapshots/publication, independent range and performance limits are visible.
- Detailed fields and traces live in linked support layers rather than being copied into the brief.

Any model correction required by projection must create a new analytical version and freeze before revising the affected brief. Version 2 contains no cross-instrument architecture recommendation; it returns generated samples and bounded calculations only.
